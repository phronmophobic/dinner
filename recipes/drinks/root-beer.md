# Root Beer

## Yield
- 1 gallon

## Ingredients

### Root beer "tea" (flavor base)
- 3 quarts (2.8 L) water (you will top up to 1 gallon later)
- 1 oz (28 g) dried sassafras root bark **or** 1–2 tsp sassafras extract (see notes)
- 0.5 oz (14 g) dried sarsaparilla root
- 1 cinnamon stick
- optional: 6 whole cloves (go higher only if you love clove)
- 1 tsp dried ginger **or** 10–15 g fresh ginger, sliced
- optional: 1 strip orange or lemon peel (no white pith)
- 1 whole star anise pod (replacement for licorice root)

### Sweetener
- 1.5 cups (300–400 g) granulated sugar, to taste  
  - Optional: replace 1/2 cup with molasses or dark brown sugar for a deeper flavor

### Finishing (add after cooling)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Optional: 1/8–1/4 tsp wintergreen extract (very strong—start low)

## Equipment
- Sous vide circulator + large pot/tub
- Heat-safe bag (weighted) **or** heat-safe jar
- Fine mesh strainer / cheesecloth
- Keg + CO₂ for force carbonation

## Instructions

1. **Sous vide extract**
   - Combine all "tea" ingredients (water + roots/spices/peel) in a bag or jar.
   - Sous vide at **160°F / 71°C for 1.5–2 hours**.

2. **Strain**
   - Strain out all solids into a pot or large pitcher.

3. **Dissolve sugar**
   - While still warm, stir in sugar (and optional molasses/brown sugar) until fully dissolved.

4. **Cool, then finish**
   - Chill the concentrate thoroughly.
   - Stir in **vanilla**, a **pinch of salt**, and any **extracts** (sassafras extract and/or wintergreen) **after cooling**.

5. **Top up**
   - Add cold water to reach **exactly 1 gallon (3.8 L)** total volume.

6. **Force carbonate**
   - Transfer to a sanitized keg and chill to **34–38°F (1–3°C)**.
   - Carbonate to **2.5–3.0 volumes CO₂** (root beer is usually on the higher side).
     - Common method: **~18-20 psi for ~3 days** cold, then reduce to serving pressure.
   - Pour gently (root beer foams easily).
